About Me

Behind Every Frame,
A Story.

I am Jones V Anand, a Director of Photography based in Chennai, India. I am passionate about visual storytelling, cinematography, photography and filmmaking.

I have experience working in the Tamil film industry and have worked as an Assistant Director of Photography under cinematographers U. K. Senthil Kumar and S. Saravanan.

My work focuses on camera operation, lighting, composition and camera movements. I aim to transform creative ideas into compelling cinematic experiences by collaborating with directors and production teams.
